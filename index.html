A possible first experiment

When do language models follow the wrong instruction?

I plan to explore how a local language model responds when a prompt contains conflicting instructions or misleading context. A useful first study would define a small test set, specify expected behavior in advance, score responses consistently, and document failure cases and limitations.

The goal is not to prove a broad safety claim from a few prompts, but to make a narrow question testable and report what the evidence does—and does not—show.
Proposed experiment · Not yet reported
